How to Make a Instagram Post

Assembly time: 5 minutes
Awesome rating: 100% awesome

Are you wondering how to make a Instagram Post? It's really fun to make. Why don't you try to make it yourself? Follow the steps and you'll learn how to make an amazing post!

  • Things You'll Need

  • An Instagram account
  • A picture or functioning phone camera

  1. Open the Instagram app on your Android, iPhone, or iPad.

    Tap the Instagram app icon, which is the multicolored camera icon on your phone or tablet.

  2. Tap the +.

    This plus sign is at the top-left corner of the Instagram app. This opens your Instagram camera to the Story screen by default. At the bottom, you'll see the option to select a post type.

  3. Take or select a photo or video.

    If you want to upload a photo you've already taken, you can select it from your phone or tablet's gallery. If you'd rather capture a new photo, tap the camera icon to open the camera viewfinder.

  4. Edit your post.

    You can add audio, text, overlays, and filters to your post using the options at the bottom of the screen. If you'd like to adjust the colors, brightness, and positioning of your post, tap the Edit button and select an editing tool.

  5. Add a caption.

    Tap the text field at the top of the screen, then type in the caption that you want to use.

  6. Tap Share.

    This posts your photo to your Instagram page.

  7. Need More Help?

    If you need more help, don't worry. Here is a YouTube video on the whole process.
